Minder turns your phone into a pair of eyes that watch you work.

Prop your phone up, slide to focus, and get to it. The eyes stay with you: they blink, glance around, raise an eyebrow, look proud when you've been going a while, and get very suspicious if you pick your phone up mid-session.

It's body doubling for people with ADHD, or anyone who works better when someone's watching. No lists, no streak guilt, no feeds. Just a black screen and two eyes that care whether you're working.

WHAT THE EYES DO
• Blink, look around, and react with a dozen expressions: attentive, focused, happy, proud, curious, suspicious, stern, sleepy and more
• Notice when the phone is picked up or moved during a session
• Optional: follow your face using the front camera, look for you when you leave, and cheer up when you come back. Nothing is recorded or leaves the phone
• Check in every 15, 25, 45 or 60 minutes with a proud look and a gentle buzz
• Get sleepy, then fall asleep, when you leave them alone

MAKE THEM YOURS
• Nine iris colours, or odd eyes with a different colour in each
• Round, almond or tall eyes
• Round, cat, heart or star pupils
• Bold, thin, bushy or no eyebrows
• Classic, tired or glowing "void" whites
• Size, spacing, glow and lashes
• Presets: Classic, Robot, Cat, Crush, Night owl, Grump, or roll the dice

ALSO
• Keeps the screen awake while you work
• Tracks focus minutes per day, your week at a glance, and your streak
• No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your phone
