Minder turns your phone into a pair of eyes that watch you work.

Prop your phone up, slide to focus, and get to it. The eyes stay with you: they blink, glance around, raise an eyebrow, look proud when you've been going a while, and get very suspicious if you pick your phone up mid-session.

It's body doubling for people with ADHD, or anyone who works better when someone's watching. No lists, no streak guilt, no feeds. Just a black screen and two eyes that care whether you're working.

Minder is free. The eyes, the focus timer, your week and the check-ins are free forever. Minder Pro is a one-time unlock for the full eye designer and Follow my face.
