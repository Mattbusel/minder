Minder turns your phone into a pair of eyes that watch you work.

Prop your phone up, slide to focus, and get to it. The eyes stay with you: they blink, glance around, raise an eyebrow, look proud when you've been going a while, and get very suspicious if you pick your phone up mid-session.

It's body doubling for people with ADHD, or anyone who works better when someone's watching. A black screen, two eyes, and a reason to keep going.

FREE, AND BUILT TO BRING YOU BACK
• Slide to focus; a timer and gentle check-ins every 15, 25, 45 or 60 minutes
• A daily goal ring and a streak, with one Streak Shield every month for the day life gets in the way
• A monthly challenge to chase, with a trophy when you get there
• Tag each session (Work, Study, Read, Write, Create) and plan it: open-ended, 25, 50 or 90 minutes
• A 5-minute break when you finish, while the eyes doze off too
• A Lock Screen and Dynamic Island timer while you focus
• Widgets for the Home Screen, Lock Screen and StandBy; tap one to start focusing
• A Start focusing control for Control Center, the Lock Screen and the Action button (iOS 18)
• Brown noise to focus to, made on the phone
• Your week, and where the time went, by tag
• Siri and Shortcuts: "Start focusing in Minder" and "How long have I focused in Minder"
